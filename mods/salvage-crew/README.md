# Salvage

This mod lives in its own repository: **[cp2077-salvage-crew](https://github.com/penicius-code/cp2077-salvage-crew)**.

Salvage androids and a salvage drone collect loot from downed enemies after a fight; send them away to sell or recycle their cargo. Single-player only.

|---|---|
